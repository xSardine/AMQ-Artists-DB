About

AnisongDB is an anime song database used for finding and listening to anime songs. Search by anime, song name, artist, composer, season, or database ID. Open a song's info panel to explore artist credits, aliases, group memberships, and line-ups.

It mirrors AMQ's song database and follows their rules for what can be uploaded as a song. This means mistakes or other issues should be reported to AMQ instead of here. The database is manually synced about once per month.
